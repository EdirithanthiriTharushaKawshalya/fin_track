🚀 FinTrack v3.0.0 – The "Financial Intelligence" Stable Release

Overview
FinTrack v3.0.0 marks our transition from a transaction logger to a full-scale "Financial Command Center." This major "Stable" update introduces our next-generation visual language, sophisticated debt intelligence, and enterprise-grade data portability tools designed for the modern wealth-builder.

What’s New

Next-Gen "Glassmorphism 2.0" UI
Aesthetic Command Center: A complete visual overhaul featuring adaptive glows, grid backgrounds, and futuristic translucent elements that merge high-end aesthetics with practical engineering.
Optimized for Clarity: Redesigned typography and spacing to ensure your financial data is readable at a glance, whether in high-contrast light or deep-space dark mode.

IOU Intelligence (Owes & Owed)
Sophisticated Debt Management: A new dedicated engine to track "Borrowed" and "Lent" funds. Keep records of personal transactions with precise due dates and settlement tracking.
Unified Planning: Debts are now integrated directly into your "Strategic Planning" view, providing a 360-degree view of your actual net worth.

Enterprise Data Portability
Native CSV Export: Your data belongs to you. Export your entire transaction history to CSV format with a single tap for professional auditing or local backup.
Seamless Sharing: Integrated with native system share sheets to instantly send your financial reports to email, cloud storage, or spreadsheet apps.

Strategic Wealth Planning
Advanced Goal Engine: Track saving targets for vacations, emergencies, or large purchases with real-time progress calculations and intelligent deadline monitoring.
Master Balance Sync: Manage cash, bank accounts, and investments in one unified view. Effortlessly monitor your total liquidity across all asset classes.

Visual Intelligence & Analytics
Solid Pie Charts: High-fidelity, interactive data visualization that lets you dive deep into spending patterns.
Categorical Deep-Dives: New interactive focus states allow you to analyze individual categories, revealing the exact number of records and percentage impact on your monthly budget.

Technical Details
Core Architecture: Re-engineered with Flutter 3.8+, Riverpod 3.1, and Firebase Firestore for ultimate stability and real-time synchronization.
Lead Architect & UI Designer: Tharusha Kawshalya.
Localization: Standardized en_LK formatting with intelligent currency parsing.

How to Update
Download the v3.0.0 Stable APK from the official landing page or GitHub repository.
Install the file (Allow "Unknown Sources" if prompted).
Log in to your FinTrack account to instantly sync your data to the new next-gen architecture.
